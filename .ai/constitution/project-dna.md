# Project DNA

Mission: Plan and create an approachable, aviation-authentic, 2D top-down airport management game in Unreal Engine.

Archetype: document-project

Engineering temperament: balanced autonomy with explicit review gates.
<!-- ai-project-init:end managed -->

## Product identity

- Grow a living airport from a grass airstrip to a major airport in the player's chosen specialization.
- Connect visible construction, airport operations, business decisions, aviation rules, and watchable individual stories.
- Work planning-first and evidence-first; preserve the 2D-only direction.
