# Phase 4.5 Adversarial Review

**Status:** Required countermeasures accepted  
**Date:** 2026-07-26  
**Scope:** CT-05 completeness and visual-content parity

## Review question

Could the project retain its accepted palette and rounded panels while still
claiming concept fidelity without implementing the content that makes airport
operations visible?

## Findings and countermeasures

### A named screenshot could be the wrong state

VA-01 currently compares a mature overview against new-airport creation,
VA-02 compares proposal mode against committed progress, and VA-07 compares a
capability map against a phase prompt. CT-05 requires equivalent lifecycle
state, scale, and zoom metadata before comparison evidence is accepted.

### Text could impersonate spatial feedback

Construction validity, passenger routes, services, weather, closures, and
incident response currently exist largely as prose. Each spatial condition now
requires a visible world counterpart linked to its inspector.

### Primitive rectangles could be counted as finished art

Proxy existence proves performance and ownership, not recognizable content.
Required props, people, bags, vehicles, equipment, icons, markings, and
patterns need reviewed project-owned assets and provenance.

### A list could be called a timetable

Uniform daily summary cards do not expose time geometry, duration, gaps, or
localized conflicts. Flight cards must be positioned by authoritative
day/minute/gate data.

### A tiny aircraft could satisfy turnaround state

An aircraft record and two service labels do not make the aircraft the visual
anchor. Close-operations framing, service choreography, safety zones, and a
dependency timeline are explicit acceptance requirements.

### Incident panels could conceal an empty world

Weather and lifecycle text do not prove the affected runway, aircraft,
closure, route, or responding vehicles are visible. Same-frame world/UI
evidence is required.

### Staged progression could hide the promised equal paths

The first three specializations cannot substitute for the six-path capability
map. Later branches must be visible as honest locked destinations with equal
weight, not omitted or presented as implemented gameplay.

### Responsive and packaging success could be mistaken for visual quality

No-clipping, cooking, and socket checks remain necessary but are not visual
parity. CT-05 separately requires content completeness and image inspection.

## Exit condition

The remediation cannot close while any CT-05 row remains `Absent`,
`Text-only`, or `Schematic`, except explicitly approved `Future-locked`
gameplay destinations.
