# Phase 6 Adversarial Review

**Status:** Technical controls passed; owner exit gates open
**Date:** 2026-07-28
**Scope:** PI-09 Major progression, scale systems, large aircraft, incidents,
persistence, presentation, and packaging

## Review question

Could Phase 6 appear to unlock Major and display a large airport while the
underlying runway, capacity, aircraft, path independence, incident recovery,
save continuity, or maximum-scale behavior remains decorative?

## Risks and required countermeasures

### Major could be another display-only band

Major derives from authoritative AP, qualifying days, ratings, tenant
relationships, path operations, facilities, events, and shared-resource
evidence. Six fixtures must complete through commands and ordinary systems.

### Major could funnel every path into a passenger hub

Each non-Passenger fixture explicitly excludes terminal, passenger operator,
baggage, concession, and wide-body content unless its own path requires it.
AP-source audits prove 100 AP remains reachable without unrelated facilities.

### Capability loss could erase hours of progression

Earned and operational bands are separate. Requirement loss creates
grace/recovery and may suspend operations, but never deletes the earned band,
achievement, or prior evidence.

### Parallel runways could be decorative markings

Each end owns heading-derived identity, declared capability, reservation,
configuration, weather/noise recommendation, occupancy, closure, crossing,
wake, and queue behavior. Tests must expose conflicting reservations and
prove independent safe operation.

### The 787 could be a resized sprite with no operational consequences

Scheduling validates runway, taxi, stand, passenger, baggage, emergency, and
service compatibility. Turnaround creates ordinary quantity-based tasks and
cannot complete until passenger/bag/service reconciliation passes.

### High-capacity systems could use separate summary counters

Phase 6 links existing passenger, bag, flight, task, shipment, tenant, rating,
route, vehicle, and economy records. Aggregate diagnostics are derived views,
not alternate authority.

### A severe incident could be random punishment or irreversible failure

Aircraft loss requires a disclosed risk and acknowledged decision/policy.
Seeded outcomes retain causal evidence. Bounded closure, response,
investigation, repair, recovery funding, and safe reopening must work through
ordinary systems and save at every lifecycle boundary.

### Aggregation could silently delete identity or alter outcomes

Stable-ID buckets may stagger work only within declared tolerances. Pooled
visuals never change authoritative totals, ordering, queues, reservations, or
checksums. Maximum fixtures compare aggregated and non-aggregated outcomes.

### Schema 7 could corrupt accepted saves

Schemas 1-6 migrate with empty Phase 6 state and preserved legacy truth.
Migration, corrupt references, future versions, arbitrary save boundaries,
and uninterrupted/resumed checksums are retained fixtures.

### New UI could repeat the text-heavy prototype

Dedicated concise views, reusable Riverbend templates, spatial world
counterparts, equivalent-state Figma boards, copy-density review, five-scale
captures, visible-mouse checks, and final visual approval are required.

### Shipping could omit assets or include tools

Source and IoStore inventories must match. Scans require zero runtime string
loads, required 3D assets, editor/MCP/test dependencies, unapproved aircraft,
real operator branding, or network requirements. Offline Shipping smoke must
exercise the Phase 6 path.

## Exit condition

Focused and full automation, six independent Major fixtures,
serious-incident recovery, deterministic maximum-scale evidence, repository
visual comparisons, clean Development and Shipping packages, content and
provenance inventory, and dependency scans pass.

The adversarial technical review is closed. Formal phase exit remains open
until the six Phase 5 and six Phase 6 owner journeys, the final owner visual
checkpoint, and the bounded 15-minute maximum-load observation are recorded.
