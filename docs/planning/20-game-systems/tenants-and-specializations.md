# GS-12: Tenants and Airport Specializations

**Status:** Approved baseline  
**Owner:** Tenants and strategic identity  
**Last updated:** 2026-07-25

## Outcome and scope

Players can build a successful general-aviation, flight-school, charter, cargo, passenger, or mixed airport. This specification owns tenant offers, leases, requirements, satisfaction, specialization capability, and coexistence. No path is merely a temporary step toward an international passenger hub.

## Tenant contract

A tenant offer states business type, footprint or facility needs, opening cost, rent/revenue model, service expectations, minimum capability, operating pattern, satisfaction factors, and contract term. Types include fixed-base operator, flight school, charter operator, passenger operator, cargo operator, maintenance service, fuel provider, rental-car company, concessions, and supporting airport businesses.

The player reviews plain-language requirements before signing. Accepted tenants receive assigned facilities and optional exclusive areas. Missing required capability blocks opening; later loss creates a grace period and specific recovery tasks rather than instant termination.

## Specialization

Specialization is emergent from active capability and sustained operation, not a one-time class selection. Each path has four capability bands: `Established`, `Regional`, `Advanced`, and `Major`. Capability checks use path-specific facilities, throughput, service reliability, safety, and tenant relationships.

All paths unlock meaningful late-game content:

- GA: diverse based aircraft, fly-ins, hangars, maintenance, and community activity;
- flight school: training fleet, pattern scheduling, instruction facilities, and events;
- charter: premium handling, irregular schedules, and business aviation;
- cargo: warehouses, freight equipment, specialist contracts, and night operations;
- passenger: terminal scale, connections, baggage, and scheduled operators;
- mixed: shared-resource policy and conflict management, with no throughput penalty merely for being mixed.

Progress in one path never erases another. Players can change emphasis gradually.

## Tenant satisfaction

Tenant satisfaction uses contract compliance, controllable delays, assigned-space quality, service availability, fees, and communication during disruptions. It is visible and explained. Low satisfaction first reduces offers or triggers a recovery request. Termination requires sustained failure after warnings and cannot remove the airport's recovery options.

## Acceptance

Tests must prove each specialization can reach Major without passenger-hub requirements, mixed-airport coexistence, readable contract validation, grace and recovery, tenant-space assignment, and save/load. A content audit must map every unlock and achievement to at least one viable path or universal capability.
