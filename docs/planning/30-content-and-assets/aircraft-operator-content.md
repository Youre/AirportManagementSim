# CT-02: Aircraft and Operator Content Catalog

**Status:** Approved baseline  
**Owner:** Aviation content  
**Last updated:** 2026-07-25

## Outcome and scope

The aircraft roster uses recognizable real model names with accurate operational differences and fictional operators that create personality without real-airline branding. This specification owns roster selection, operator archetypes, liveries, tail-number rules, accuracy review, and content coverage.

## Aircraft roster

The first playable vertical slice requires one light piston aircraft. The GA phase expands to at least four roles: basic trainer, touring piston, business turboprop, and business jet. Later phases add regional passenger, narrow-body passenger, cargo-capable variants, and one wide-body only after scale validation.

Roster selection prioritizes:

- clear top-down silhouette and usable approved 2D art;
- distinct operational role rather than cosmetic duplication;
- documented dimensions and capability;
- runway/stand/service variety;
- relevance to contemporary civil aviation;
- balanced coverage across viable specializations.

Military aircraft are not core scheduled content. Historical or military visitors require an event-specific content and tone review.

## Fictional operators

Operators use fictional names, logos, liveries, home regions, service preferences, fleet pools, offer patterns, and relationship text. Archetypes include local flying club, flight school, regional commuter, low-cost passenger carrier, full-service carrier, charter company, express freight, heavy cargo, and independent corporate operator.

Fictional branding must not imitate a real operator's name, logo, trade dress, or signature livery. Tail-number format may resemble regional civil registration patterns but must avoid knowingly duplicating famous or sensitive aircraft.

## Accuracy and rights

Every aircraft definition cites sources for model identity, dimensions, typical capacity range, and broad runway/performance category. Exact proprietary manuals are not required, but invented precision is prohibited. The catalog records whether real model-name use, source art, and derivative livery use have passed rights review.

If a model cannot clear accuracy or rights review, it is replaced by a fictionalized type before release; save migration maps the old content ID to the replacement.

## Coverage validation

Each aircraft must have a compatible runway, taxi path, stand, service set, role, operator, sprite directions, inspect text, radio call-sign format, and offer template. Each specialization phase defines a minimum viable roster in PI-01. Content tests create and complete one flight for every aircraft/role combination.
