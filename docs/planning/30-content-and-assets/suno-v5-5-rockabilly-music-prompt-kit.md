# Riverbend Airport — Suno v5.5 Rockabilly and Rock-and-Roll Music Prompts

This companion prompt bank adds a more guitar- and slap-bass-forward branch
to the [piano-led early rock-and-roll prompt kit](suno-v5-5-music-prompt-kit.md).
It is for optional instrumental music exploration only. It does not alter the
offline speech, captions-first, mix-group, provenance, or shipping-rights
requirements in [CT-03](radio-audio-speech.md).

All prompts direct original instrumental music. Do not use named performers,
source tracks, recognizable melodies, lyrics, spoken word, real-operator
branding, or unreviewed samples.

## M17 — Cargo Ramp Rockabilly

> Instrumental rockabilly airport-operations cue, 118 BPM. An original small-combo groove with slap upright bass, crisp snare-and-brush shuffle, clean twang rhythm guitar, upright piano accents, and a lightly muted tenor sax answering the hook. Purposeful cargo trucks, warehouse docks, and freighter turnarounds: busy, competent, never frantic. Keep the melody concise and hummable; include a four-bar low-density break for radio and captions; warm analog mix and a clear loop-friendly ending. Instrumental only, no vocals, no spoken word, no artist imitation, no distortion, no arena rock, no country ballad, no crowd or airport recordings, no orchestral score.

## Retention gate

Before retaining any generated audio, record the generator/service, exact
prompt, request identity or seed, duration, output checksum, post-processing,
intended mix-group use, and approval or rejection. Preserve source masters
outside cooked content and import only an approved derived asset.
