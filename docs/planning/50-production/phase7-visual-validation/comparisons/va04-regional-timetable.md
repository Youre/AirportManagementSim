# VA-04 Visual Comparison — Regional Timetable

**Equivalent state:** Seven-day recurring schedule with a selected regional
flight and a pending offer  
**Board:** [actual versus concept](va04-regional-timetable.png)

## Review

- Seven labeled days, time grid, flight blocks, gate/stand rows, selected
  contract, aircraft/operator identity, feasibility, weather, and time controls
  reproduce the concept's planning purpose.
- Flight blocks use labels and patterns in addition to color. Selection and
  compatibility remain unambiguous.
- The dense planning surface uses the same navy/teal/amber/coral rounded
  language as the world views without introducing a separate visual system.

## Highest-impact differences

1. The implementation is more information-dense and typographically compact
   than the concept's broad, highly illustrated flight cards.
2. The concept uses larger aircraft silhouettes and more decorative operator
   branding.
3. The implementation's actual 21-flight schedule produces a busier grid than
   the concept placeholder timetable.

## Intentional differences

- Exact slots, weather window, resource conflicts, feasibility, capacity, and
  rewards come from the deterministic simulation.
- Readable labels and redundant state take priority over concept-only logo
  decoration.

## Bounded correction completed

Regional Operations no longer auto-selects Advanced or Major. Timetable,
Overview, Capabilities, Advanced, and Major are explicit peer destinations
with a persistent Back action.
