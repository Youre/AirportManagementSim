# VA-07 Visual Comparison — Capability Progression

**Equivalent state:** Riverbend capability map with six independent,
combinable paths and current evidence  
**Board:** [actual versus concept](va07-progression.png)

## Review

- General Aviation, Flight School, Charter, Cargo, Passenger, and Mixed have
  equal-weight cards around the player's airport.
- Established, Regional, Advanced, and Major are explicit for every path;
  status words accompany color.
- Suggested objectives, current evidence, spatial/business effect, next
  capability, reward/outcome, and the non-exclusive combination rule are
  present.
- The radial relationship, navy card system, cyan/green progress, amber
  attention, and accessible type retain the concept's approachable tone.

## Highest-impact differences

1. The concept uses detailed airport thumbnails and larger bespoke
   specialization illustrations; the implementation uses the approved icon
   atlas and concise evidence text.
2. The implementation shows more literal band/status words and less decorative
   reward art.
3. The selected-path inspector is narrower and more text-forward than the
   concept's large Cargo example card.

## Intentional differences

- Progression is capability-based and non-exclusive. It does not recreate the
  concept's suggestion of a single chosen class.
- Major remains locked or ready according to real Phase 6 evidence rather than
  illustrative values.

The proof fixture now re-applies the Capabilities destination after the
incident state, and Advanced/Major no longer replace sibling content
