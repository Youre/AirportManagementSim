## Highest-impact mismatches

1. VA-03 has much richer terminal architecture, furniture, passengers, service points, and interior surface hierarchy than the current schematic runtime terminal.
2. VA-03 makes passenger routes, occupant movement, and operational exceptions immediately readable; those details are too small at the full-airport zoom and need stronger zoom-level presentation.
3. The runtime airport and terminal materials remain flatter and less densely dressed than the concept, especially at the curb, apron, gates, and surrounding landscape.

## Intentional differences

- The runtime deliberately preserves the entire airport and the current camera frame while Terminal is open, whereas VA-03 is a tightly framed terminal composition. The player zooms into the same world rather than navigating to that framing as another screen.

## Next bounded iteration

- Expected visual change: Denser strict-nadir furnishings, clearer room/route hierarchy, and localized operational animation become readable as the player zooms into the terminal.
- Acceptance observation: The airport remains one persistent world at every zoom, while the close terminal frame approaches VA-03's content density and operational readability.
