## Highest-impact mismatches

1. VA-01 devotes more of the playable composition to the terminal and its
   immediate curb/apron context; runtime keeps the full mature airport in one
   camera frame, although the terminal is now a readable 240x144-meter campus.
2. VA-01 has much denser landscaping, vehicles, people, and facility dressing;
   the runtime campus remains comparatively sparse and diagrammatic.
3. VA-01 uses a large contextual aircraft inspector at the bottom; runtime
   retains the compact radio/navigation strip in this deterministic state.

## Intentional differences

- The terminal stays permanently roofless so its interior remains visible on
  the only airport canvas; the concept depicts a conventional exterior roof.
- Runtime positions, state, facilities, and values come from the deterministic
  Phase 4 fixture rather than incidental concept values.
