## Highest-impact mismatches

1. The implementation remains substantially less landscaped and populated than
   VA-01, especially around the terminal forecourt and airport perimeter.
2. The runway is readable at the top of the operational frame, but the current
   map-dominant framing does not show both thresholds as prominently as VA-01.
3. Support facilities use clean procedural silhouettes and markings rather
   than the concept's richer authored roof and equipment detail.

## Intentional differences

- The terminal stays permanently roofless so its spatial interior remains
  playable on the single airport canvas.
- The implementation retains the project's compact rail and caption bar rather
  than copying incidental concept controls.
