## Highest-impact mismatches

1. The runway, connected airside and landside network, facilities, landscaping,
   and mixed activity read as one airport. The project-owned 2D site remains
   less spatially dense than the concept's apron, perimeter, and roadway.
2. Aircraft and Regional Terminal tabs now switch a real selected-entity
   inspector. Each mode swaps identity art and query-backed operational
   evidence while the cyan world focus moves between the aircraft and terminal.
   The terminal variant is captured separately so its larger focus treatment
   and facility-specific content can be inspected directly.
3. The icon-plus-label tool rail and separated activity rail preserve the
   concept's scan order. Tools retain a selected state, while the inspector's
   contextual actions open the timetable and capability surfaces. Optional
   tool-specific spatial-filter drawers are not yet authored.

## Intentional differences

- The implementation shows the authored regional airport and real Phase 4
  values rather than copying the concept's mature layout, aircraft, totals, or
  service inventory.
