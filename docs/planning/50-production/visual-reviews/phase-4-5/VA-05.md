## Highest-impact mismatches

1. The close camera, aircraft anchor, assigned fuel vehicle, ramp worker, cones,
   patterned exclusion zone, and two authored approach arrows now establish
   safe service choreography without relying on color alone.
2. The six-part timeline now names parallel inspection/fueling, shows
   authoritative active percentages, progress bars, the shared readiness
   dependency, automatic dispatch, and predicted completion. It remains
   visually compressed compared with the concept's lower task tray.
3. Cooker-visible aircraft art, operator/tail/call sign, role, four-place
   capacity, stand, arrival/ready schedule, and predicted readiness are
   present. The dispatch card explicitly says the player sets priority rather
   than steering; Phase 1 correctly exposes no manual-driving command.

## Intentional differences

- Phase 1 has only inspection and fueling tasks for a Cessna 152. The UI does
  not invent boarding, baggage, catering, cleaning, or pushback gameplay from
  the larger-aircraft concept.
