## Highest-impact mismatches

1. The mature airport stays active through the protected-state capture. The
   enlarged affected aircraft, red hatched closure, cones, and protected-area
   treatment make the incident the first world focal point, although the
   closure is less integrated with the runway edge than the concept.
2. Six continuous coral connectors, directional markers, and three staged
   response vehicles now trace the response from origin to the affected
   aircraft. The concept still has a more polished route ribbon and denser
   vehicle staging.
3. The project-owned weather icon, 54 rain streaks, cool dark treatment,
   conditions, confidence, and forecast create a clear poor-weather state.
   Separate cooker-visible runway, taxiway, and apron wet layers add localized
   cool sheen below the closure and route. The stylized Paper2D result remains
   less reflective and fog-dense than the painted concept.

  never depicts human harm.
